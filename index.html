How a build runs

A 10 marla complete house, start to keys. Smaller jobs skip the stages they don't need.

  1. 1

    Site visit

    We check the plot, levels, soil and access, and note what your society allows.

    Days 1 to 3
  2. 2

    Drawings and approval

    Architectural and structural drawings, then submission to the approving authority.

    Weeks 1 to 6
  3. 3

    BOQ and contract

    An itemised bill of quantities and a fixed rate per sq ft, signed before work starts.

    Week 6
  4. 4

    Grey structure

    Excavation, footings, columns, beams and slabs. Each slab is cured for 28 days.

    Months 2 to 7
  5. 5

    Finishing

    Tiles, woodwork, wiring, plumbing, paint and fixtures, done room by room.

    Months 7 to 12
  6. 6

    Handover

    A snag-list walk-through, the keys, as-built drawings and a 12-month defects warranty.

    Month 13
